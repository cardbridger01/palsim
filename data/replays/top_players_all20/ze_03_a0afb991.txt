PROSE SUMMARY (context, not a substitute for the structured log above)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The longest and grindiest game in the whole 20-game pull, decided by deck-out rather than a clean
knockout. Both players ground through 17 full turns of trades and Zoe's Strategy recursion, but
the decisive edge was tempo: bel's repeated Pengullet-explode plays (three separate 1600-damage
board wipes) kept resetting XMG's board while bel's Elphidran/Elphidran Aqua/Chillet core chipped
away at life in clean 2-life increments (10->8->6->4->2->0 across turns 3, 5, 9, 16, and 17). XMG
hit back three times through turns 12-14 (Chillet, Chillet, Chillet again) taking bel from 10 down
to 4, but a dense defensive package (Blazehowl Noct and Cryolinx doing double duty as both
attack-side and defense-side interrupts) kept that from going further. XMG's deck ran out at the
same moment their life did — the UI credits the deck-out as the official win condition, but
either reading tells the same story: XMG simply ran out of resources to keep pace.
