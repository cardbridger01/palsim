----------------------------------------------------------------------
A 13-true-turn game, extracted directly from the raw event log rather than the curated view (which
split it into at least 28 displayed boxes). Pingu opened aggressively with Pengullet, taking
redpurple from 10 to 4 life across true turns 3-5 while trading Pals back and forth with redpurple's
Suzaku/Shadowbeak/Foxparks package (redpurple's Shadowbeak repeatedly "butchered" a Pingu Pal on
resolution, costing Pingu a Pengullet, two Chillets, and two Jormuntides over the game). A true
turn 8 Jormuntide swing put redpurple to 1 life, and although redpurple stabilized the board for
several turns with Suzaku/Leezpunk/Menasting and repeated Pyrin Noct/Foxparks/Blazehowl interrupts,
they were never able to find a fifth point of damage against Pingu (who sat at 7 life from true
turn 6 onward, protected by Lucky Pal reveals and full-form interrupts) and surrendered from a
precarious 1-life, thinning-resources position rather than play it out. Final score 7-1 to Pingu.
No damage is unaccounted for on either side.
