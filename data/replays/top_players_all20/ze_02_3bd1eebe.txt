PROSE SUMMARY (context, not a substitute for the structured log above)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A 13-turn game where redpurple opened aggressively with two small early attackers (Foxparks,
Leezpunk) that chipped bel down from 10 to 7 life in the first two turns, then pivoted into a
value/control shell (Astegon board wipe on turn 3, Reptyro + weapon package from turn 8 on).
bel answered with steady Chillet/Elphidran Aqua attacks (each interrupted or landing for 2),
grinding redpurple from 10 down to 6 while never taking further damage after turn 2 — helped by
two separate Lucky Pal cancels on turns 7 and 10 that denied redpurple's counter-pressure. Unlike
match 1, both final life totals are fully explained by the log's own damage lines, and redpurple
conceded from a materially worse board/tempo position (bel had two live threats plus a
newly-deployed second Jormuntide) rather than from a near-lethal life total.
